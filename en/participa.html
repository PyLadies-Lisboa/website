Code of Conduct

PyLadies Lisboa follows the PyLadies Global Code of Conduct.

We want everyone who takes part to feel welcome, whatever their background and identity. We want the community to be a space for sharing and growth, built on openness, care and respect.

We ask for kindness and good faith at all our events and spaces, in person and online, and we do not tolerate harassment, discrimination or exclusion.

If anything makes you uncomfortable, talk to us at lisboa@pyladies.com or to any member of the organising team.
