Hello, Lisboa!

PyLadies Lisboa is a community for women and non-binary people who want to learn, use and lead in Python.

For every level: whether you're taking your first steps, have been coding for years or have never heard of Python, there's a place for you.

About us

A community to learn together

We are the Lisbon chapter of PyLadies, the international community that wants to make the Python world more welcoming and accessible to women and non-binary people. We create a space to learn, share and grow, with no pressure. Beginner, expert or only just heard of Python? There's a place for you. Come along: the door's open.

Code of Conduct

PyLadies Lisboa follows the PyLadies Global Code of Conduct.

We want everyone who takes part to feel welcome, whatever their background and identity. We want the community to be a space for sharing and growth, built on openness, care and respect.

We ask for kindness and good faith at all our events and spaces, in person and online, and we do not tolerate harassment, discrimination or exclusion.

If anything makes you uncomfortable, talk to us at lisboa@pyladies.com or to any member of the organising team.
